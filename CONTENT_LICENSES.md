## VOA Learning English Course

**Everyday English with VOA** is a curated sentence-recall selection from VOA Learning English's beginner course, _Let's Learn English - Level 1_. UtterLoop uses only sentences from the official VOA-produced conversation transcripts and retains the source URL and attribution on every included lesson and card.

VOA's [Request Our Content](https://learningenglish.voanews.com/p/6861.html) page states that VOA Learning English text is in the public domain and may be reused for educational and commercial purposes with credit to `learningenglish.voanews.com`. VOA's [Terms of Use](https://learningenglish.voanews.com/p/6021.html) likewise identifies material produced exclusively by Voice of America as public domain.

The Chinese recall prompts and UtterLoop-authored course metadata are original UtterLoop adaptations dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The in-app Voice of America public-domain label refers to the English transcript sentences, not to VOA authorship of those adaptations.

Use this attribution when redistributing the VOA course content:

> Source: VOA Learning English (`learningenglish.voanews.com`), with a link to the original lesson.

The VOA public-domain statement does not cover third-party material carried by VOA. AP, Reuters, AFP, and other agency or licensed text, photographs, graphics, audio, and video remain subject to their owners' rights. UtterLoop intentionally excludes those materials from the course. Public-domain content status also does not grant trademark rights or imply VOA endorsement of UtterLoop.
